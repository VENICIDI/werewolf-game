## 使用说明

- **尺寸**: 1024×1024 正方形，前端裁切为圆形
- **生成工具**: Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Stable Diffusion XL
- **消除 AI 味关键词**: `hand-drawn`, `visible brushstrokes`, `sketchy ink outlines`, `intentional imperfections`, `textured paper`, `pencil underdrawing`, `varying line thickness`
- **如果生成结果仍有 AI 感**: 追加 negative prompt `--no photorealistic, 3D render, smooth gradient, perfect symmetry, airbrushed, plastic skin, CGI`
